## 图表和算法

- [S2.F1](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.00395v1#S2.F1)：Figure 1: Overall Transformer dose-allocation schematic. Variable-length dose-level tokens are encoded into dose representations, while fixed-length trial context forms the query for masked multi-head cross-attention. The resulting trial representation feeds the policy and value heads.
- [S3.T1](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.00395v1#S3.T1)：Table 1: Primary five-seed v2-selection comparison with a DLT penalty. POS is the probability of selecting above the true MTD; PCA and POA are the proportions of patients allocated at and above the true MTD, respectively. RL values are mean ± \pm standard deviation across seeds; benchmark-design values are single matched simulation summaries. All methods use the same final selection rule.
- [S3.F2](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.00395v1#S3.F2)：Figure 2: Operating characteristics by number of dose levels for the toxicity-penalized RL policies ( λ DLT = 0.05 \lambda_{\mathrm{DLT}}=0.05 ) and matched benchmark designs. The RL curves show means and seed variability; the benchmark lines are single simulation summaries.
- [S3.T2](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.00395v1#S3.T2)：Table 2: Sensitivity of RL operating characteristics to the DLT penalty. POS is the probability of selecting above the true MTD; PCA and POA are the proportions of patients allocated at and above the true MTD, respectively. Values are mean ± \pm standard deviation across five seeds.
- [S3.F3](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.00395v1#S3.F3)：Figure 3: Operating characteristics by number of dose levels when the RL DLT penalty is removed ( λ DLT = 0 \lambda_{\mathrm{DLT}}=0 ). Selection accuracy increases, but mean DLTs and the fraction of patients treated above the MTD also increase.
- [S3.F4](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.00395v1#S3.F4)：Figure 4: Validation training curves for the five-seed long runs with λ DLT = 0.05 \lambda_{\mathrm{DLT}}=0.05 . Thin lines show individual seeds and thicker lines show the aggregate trajectory; horizontal reference lines are benchmark-design summaries.
- [S3.T3](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.00395v1#S3.T3)：Table 3: Optimization sensitivity sweep. Values are mean ± \pm standard deviation across three seeds. The sweep used the original rule-based selection settings for its comparator simulations; only RL rows are shown.
- [S3.F5](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.00395v1#S3.F5)：Figure 5: Validation PCS and mean DLT trajectories for the completed batch-size and entropy-coefficient sweep. Shaded regions show seed variability.
