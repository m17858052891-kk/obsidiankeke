Title:UniRec: Cross-stage Multi-Task Fusion with Preference Optimization for Cascaded Recommender Systems

 Authors:Lingyuan Kong, Jiaqi Cui, Fanjiao Zeng, Congqi Wang, Yu Li, Yuan Cheng, Jingxin Liu, Xiaoshuang Chen, Kaiqiao Zhan View a PDF of the paper titled UniRec: Cross-stage Multi-Task Fusion with Preference Optimization for Cascaded Recommender Systems, by Lingyuan Kong and 8 other authors
 View PDF
 HTML (experimental)

 Abstract:Industrial recommender systems use cascaded stages with different candidate spaces, data distributions, and latency constraints. Each stage include multi-objective prediction and multi-task fusion (MTF) modules. Existing MTF methods typically design and optimize the fusion module independently for each stage, which can lead to inconsistent preferences across stages. Although cross-stage coordination methods have been proposed, they mainly transfer supervision to upstream scoring models rather than jointly optimizing the fusion modules.

We propose UniRec, a Unified Cross-stage Recommendation Fusion framework that jointly optimizes pre-ranking and ranking fusion agents. UniRec employs a coupled dual-agent architecture with partially shared embeddings and stage-specific fusion functions. It further introduces Dual-Axis Preference Optimization to combine stage-local preferences with downstream-to-upstream preference transfer. To enable stable and efficient cross-stage training, we address two practical challenges: reward-induced distribution drift and the computational cost of pairwise preference learning.

Extensive offline and online experiments on a production short-video system demonstrate consistent gains over single-stage fusion and cross-stage coordination baselines. In a nine-week online A/B test, UniRec improves total watch time by $0.675\%$ and app usage time by $0.616\%$, and active users by $0.189\%$, and has been fully deployed in production.
