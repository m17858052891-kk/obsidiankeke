# **3.样本筛选**

**Plan A (selected)**

Go by unique index, which is a combination of order_id & driver_id, in other words, a driver’s exposure of every single order will only be counted once, regardless of the time and location difference.

| **Is_answer_td** <br> | **Order_id** <br> | **Driver_id** <br> | **Exposure_id (created)** <br> |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 <br> | 1 <br> | 1 <br> | 1_1 <br> |
| 1 <br> | 2 <br> | 1 <br> | 2_1 <br> |
| 0 <br> | 3 <br> | 1 <br> | 3_1 <br> |
| 0 <br> | 4 <br> | 1 <br> | 4_1 <br> |
| 0 <br> | 5 <br> | 1 <br> | 5_1 <br> |

Good: describe the driver’s preference, or whether the driver likes the order accurately

Bad: cannot differentiate an order that a driver likes strongly vs. those a driver pick because there is no other choice, in other words, the condition of current market status is not modeled.

**Plan B**

Go by every exposure (session_id/time stamp):

| **Is_answer_td** <br> | **Order_id** <br> | **Session_id** <br> |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 <br> | 1 <br> | 1 <br> |
| 1 <br> | 2 <br> | 1 <br> |
| 0 <br> | 3 <br> | 1 <br> |
| 0 <br> | 4 <br> | 1 <br> |
| 0 <br> | 5 <br> | 1 <br> |

Good: describe the picking behavior accurately

Bad: only model the picking behavior on condition of the driver will actually pick an order from a given exposure：Y=Prob (the order got picked | the driver actually picked an order from exposure)

| Plan A Pos% <br> | Plan B Pos% <br> |
| --- | --- |
| 38% <br> | 3% <br> |
