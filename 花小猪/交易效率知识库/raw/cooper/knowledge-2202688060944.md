## 背景

对于预应答实验的三个参数的配置，考虑eta、取消时长、应答时长、接驾时长这几个指标的数据分布情况，从而限制参数配置范围来约束触发面。

## 策略参数

- 对于补勾页应答占比低的城市打包看（长沙24、泉州35、衡阳105、石家庄22、晋中71、哈尔滨16、邯郸60、吕梁194、重庆18、承德72）

- 预估应答时间：当预估应答时间>X时，本单可以触发预应答

- 开始预应答时间：在触发后，当乘客已经等待超过Y秒后，安排假司机应答

- 预应答持续时间：假司机应答后，接驾状态持续Z秒，超时后假司机取消，回到等待页

## 数据分析

### 1、预估应答时间

估应答时间X：根据线上实际预估应答时长eta的分布进行分档，决定覆盖面，预期覆盖面10%上下

| p10 <br> | p20 <br> | p30 <br> | p40 <br> | p50 <br> | p60 <br> | p70 <br> | p80 <br> | p90 <br> | p100 <br> |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 7 <br> | 9 <br> | 12 <br> | 18 <br> | 30 <br> | 43 <br> | 68 <br> | 137 <br> | 269 <br> | 1e+07 <br> |

```SQL
with eta_dur as(
select distinct param['traceid'],
cast(substr(param['predict_value'],9,6)*60 as bigint) as dur
from 
kflower_ods.ods_log_kflower_ether_hxz_main_eta_model
where param['city'] in (24,35,105,22,71,16,60,194,18,72)
)

SELECT
    percentile(dur, 0.10) AS p10,
    percentile(dur, 0.20) AS p20,
    percentile(dur, 0.30) AS p30,
    percentile(dur, 0.40) AS p40,
    percentile(dur, 0.50) AS p50,
    percentile(dur, 0.60) AS p60,
    percentile(dur, 0.70) AS p70,
    percentile(dur, 0.80) AS p80,
    percentile(dur, 0.90) AS p90,
    percentile(dur, 1.00) AS p100
FROM 
    eta_dur;
```

可以考虑X参数配置为120s/150s（这步覆盖率15%-20%，结合Y预期触发面在10%）

### 2、取消时长

发单后Y秒预应答：Y的取值根据cancel_time - call_time的分布进行分档。即在预估用户取消前后预应答（Y过长，触发面低；Y过短，影响补勾）

| p10 <br> | p20 <br> | p30 <br> | p40 <br> | p50 <br> | p60 <br> | p70 <br> | p80 <br> | p90 <br> | p100 <br> |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 15 <br> | 32 <br> | 51 <br> | 74 <br> | 99 <br> | 132 <br> | 179 <br> | 248 <br> | 386 <br> | 705090 <br> |

```SQL

WITH cancel_dur AS (
    SELECT 
        order_id, 
        passenger_id,
        MAX(UNIX_TIMESTAMP(cancel_time) - UNIX_TIMESTAMP(call_time)) AS dur -- 取消时长
    FROM 
        kflower_dw.dwd_kf_trd_order_base_di
    WHERE 
        dt BETWEEN '2024-06-17' AND '2024-06-30'
        AND is_td_cancel = 1
        AND cancel_type IN (1)
        AND source_type <> 2
        and product_1level <> 4 -- 只看小猪端内的订单
        and start_city_id in (24,35,105,22,71,16,60,194,18,72)
    GROUP BY 
        order_id, passenger_id
)

SELECT
    percentile(dur, 0.10) AS p10,
    percentile(dur, 0.20) AS p20,
    percentile(dur, 0.30) AS p30,
    percentile(dur, 0.40) AS p40,
    percentile(dur, 0.50) AS p50,
    percentile(dur, 0.60) AS p60,
    percentile(dur, 0.70) AS p70,
    percentile(dur, 0.80) AS p80,
    percentile(dur, 0.90) AS p90,
    percentile(dur, 1.00) AS p100
FROM 
    cancel_dur;
```

```SQL


WITH answer_dur AS(
select order_id, passenger_id, max(unix_timestamp(answer_time) - unix_timestamp(call_time)) as dur --应答时长
        from kflower_dw.dwd_kf_trd_order_base_di
        where dt between '2024-06-17' and '2024-06-30'
        and is_td_answer = 1
        and source_type <> 2
        and product_1level <> 4 -- 只看小猪端内的订单
        and start_city_id in (24,35,105,22,71,16,60,194,18,72)
        group by order_id, passenger_id
)       

SELECT
    percentile(dur, 0.10) AS p10,
    percentile(dur, 0.20) AS p20,
    percentile(dur, 0.30) AS p30,
    percentile(dur, 0.40) AS p40,
    percentile(dur, 0.50) AS p50,
    percentile(dur, 0.60) AS p60,
    percentile(dur, 0.70) AS p70,
    percentile(dur, 0.80) AS p80,
    percentile(dur, 0.90) AS p90,
    percentile(dur, 1.00) AS p100
FROM 
    answer_dur;
```

可以考虑Y参数配置为50s（被应答的订单中近90%在50s前已被应答，被取消的订单中30%在50s前被取消）

### 4、预估接驾时长

预应答持续Z秒：Z根据平均接驾时长（时间过长，会给乘客差体验，且影响补勾；时间过短，则无法拉伸等待时长）

| p10 <br> | p20 <br> | p30 <br> | p40 <br> | p50 <br> | p60 <br> | p70 <br> | p80 <br> | p90 <br> | p100 <br> |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 68 <br> | 110 <br> | 144 <br> | 175 <br> | 205 <br> | 235 <br> | 270 <br> | 312 <br> | 381 <br> | 5422 <br> |

```JSON
with pick_dur AS(
select distinct order_id, eta_second_pick_dur as dur --预估接驾时长
        from kflower_dw.dwd_kf_trd_order_base_di
        where dt between '2024-06-17' and '2024-06-30'
        and is_td_answer = 1
        and source_type <> 2
        and product_1level <> 4 -- 只看小猪端内的订单
        and start_city_id in (24,35,105,22,71,16,60,194,18,72)
)

SELECT
    percentile(dur, 0.10) AS p10,
    percentile(dur, 0.20) AS p20,
    percentile(dur, 0.30) AS p30,
    percentile(dur, 0.40) AS p40,
    percentile(dur, 0.50) AS p50,
    percentile(dur, 0.60) AS p60,
    percentile(dur, 0.70) AS p70,
    percentile(dur, 0.80) AS p80,
    percentile(dur, 0.90) AS p90,
    percentile(dur, 1.00) AS p100
FROM 
    pick_dur;
```

可以考虑Z参数配置为15s（Z不宜太长，考虑司机不会半路取消、万一乘客打电话等现实情况）
