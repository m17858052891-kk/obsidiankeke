## 风险点

- RCT依赖工程开发，无RCT数据无法进行模型训练、规划求解

	- RCT进行时，必须补全SDS特征（起码补全API的特征），否则无法再次获取实时特征

	- RCT进行时，相关实验需确认流量

		- 幸运盒子单勾后处理：<https://yunchou.intra.hongyibo.com.cn/experimentManage?layerId=370>

		- 随机默勾实验：<https://x.intra.hongyibo.com.cn/exp/483337963896833?globalBusinessId=120000&globalProjectId=330331944058881&expFlowTab=design>

		- 价格取整：<https://x.intra.hongyibo.com.cn/exp/487138582200321?globalBusinessId=120000&globalProjectId=330331944058881&expFlowTab=design&windowTab=undefined&dashboardTab=metricDetail&dashboardExtraTab=everyday>

		- 盒子扩展V1：[https://x.intra.hongyibo.com.cn/exp/460644233248769?globalBusinessId=120000&globalProjectId=330331944058881&expFlowTab=dashboard&dashboardTab=metricDetail](https://x.intra.hongyibo.com.cn/exp/460644233248769?globalBusinessId=120000&globalProjectId=330331944058881&expFlowTab=dashboard&dashboardTab=metricDetail)
